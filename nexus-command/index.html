Nexus reads the source —
not just the summary

Connect Nexus through MCP — the open standard for giving AI secure access to your tools — and it reaches where the work actually lives: your repos, designs, docs and databases. Its reasoning moves from description-level to schema-level — it reads the real code, not a summary of one. Live today with GitHub; Figma, Notion, Drive and databases are rolling out.

What changes when you connect it

From reading tasks to auditing the real thing

  • Reads the real artifact — the actual API code, the live DB schema, the current Figma frame, not a description of it.
  • Audits against the contract — it diffs what changed versus the interface every dependent relies on, at the schema level.
  • Catches drift at the source & acts — flags the affected owners, flips the contract to at-risk, and (with your approval) drafts the fix.
